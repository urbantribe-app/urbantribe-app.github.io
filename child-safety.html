Safety

Child safety standards

HEY STRANGER is for adults only. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1 Zero tolerance

Anywhere on HEY STRANGER (profiles, photos, plans and chats), it is forbidden to:

  • share, request, create or link to any sexual content involving anyone under 18;
  • groom, sexualise or sexually exploit a minor, or arrange to meet one;
  • use the app to contact, find or exchange contact details with minors;
  • promote, threaten or encourage any of the above.

Accounts that break these standards are banned, and we report them to the authorities as described below.

2 Adults only

  • Every member must be 18 or older. An account can't be completed with a date of birth under 18, and the date of birth can't be changed later in the app.
  • On Google Play, HEY STRANGER is for adults only, and we have asked Google Play to stop users it has identified as minors from finding or downloading the app. Google Play may not be able to identify every minor.
  • If we learn that a member is under 18, we remove the account.

3 Report a concern

In the app:

  • On a person's profile, tap ⋮ → Report. In a one-to-one chat, tap ⋮ → Report to report the other person. On a plan, tap ⋮ → Report Plan.
  • Choose Inappropriate content (or Something else) and write "child safety" and what happened in the details.
  • To stop someone contacting you, open their profile and tap ⋮ → Block. You will no longer see each other in Discover, plans, requests or chats. They are not told.

By email: write to our child safety contact, urbantribe.admin@gmail.com, with the subject "Child safety report". Include the person's name as shown in the app and what happened. Please do not send us images of abuse; describe them instead.

Email a child safety report

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police first: dial 112 in India, or call the child helpline on 1098. You can also report child sexual abuse material on India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, cybercrime.gov.in.

4 What we do with a report

  • Child safety reports are reviewed before all other reports.
  • We suspend or ban the account. This removes it from Discover and the leaderboard, cancels its open plans, withdraws its join requests and stops it signing in. Accounts that break these standards are banned.
  • We delete the content that breaks these standards.
  • We report child sexual abuse and exploitation to the police, as the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act requires, and report child sexual abuse material through India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • Before deleting anything, we keep a secure copy of the reported content and the related account details, and keep it for as long as Indian law requires (at least 180 days under the IT Rules) or longer if the authorities ask. We share it only with the authorities.
  • We co-operate with lawful requests from law enforcement.
